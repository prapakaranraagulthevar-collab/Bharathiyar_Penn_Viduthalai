# 🌟 பாரதியின் புதுமைப் பெண் — Mahakavi Bharathiyar's Vision on Women's Liberation, Safety & Empowerment

A high-performance, bilingual web application honoring **Mahakavi Subramania Bharathiyar's** revolutionary philosophy on **Penn Viduthalai (Women's Liberation)**, women's literacy, and gender equality, integrated with modern **24x7 Women Safety SOS tools**, a **Voice-Enabled AI Chatbot**, **Government Welfare Schemes**, and a **Women Business Incubation Portal**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🪶 Mahakavi Bharathiyar's Revolutionary Philosophy & Greatness
- **Sister Nivedita's Transformation (1906)**: The historic turning point where Bharati met Swami Vivekananda's disciple Sister Nivedita in Kolkata and embraced her as his spiritual Guru (*Gnana Guru*), transforming him into an ardent feminist revolutionary.
- **War on Illiteracy & Social Shackles**: How Bharati demolished century-old taboos against female literacy, child marriage, widow humiliation, and Sati with fiery poetry ("மாதர் தம்மை இழிவு செய்யும் மடமையைக் கொளுத்துவோம்!").
- **Pudumai Penn (The Modern Woman)**: Bharati's definition of womanhood characterized by upright posture, an unswerving fearless gaze, and supreme intellectual pride.
- **All Rights to All**: Bharati's visionary doctrine ("ஆணும் பெண்ணும் நிகரெனக் கொள்வதால் அறிவிலோங்கி இவ்வையம் தழைக்குமாம்!") connecting his ideals to modern women scientists, judges, pilots, and leaders.
- **Then vs. Now Comparison Matrix**: An interactive table comparing 19th-century oppression, Bharathiyar's vision, and modern reality.

### 2. 📜 Poetry Treasury & Audio Reciter
- Interactive poem library with category tabs:
  - **Pudumai Penn** (*நிமிர்ந்த நன்னடை நேர்கொண்ட பார்வை*)
  - **Penn Viduthalai Kummi** (*ஏட்டையும் பெண்கள் தொடுவது தீமை*)
  - **Samathuvam / Penmai Vaazhga** (*பெண்மை வாழ்கவென்று கூத்திடுவோமடா*)
  - **Achamillai Achamillai** (*அச்சமில்லை அச்சமில்லை அச்சமென்பதில்லையே*)
- Line-by-line Tamil verses with poetic English translations.
- Built-in Text-to-Speech audio recitation for every poem.
- One-click copy with instant feedback.

### 3. 🚨 24x7 Women Safety Command Hub & Emergency SOS
- **Big Animated Emergency SOS Button**:
  - Live **Audible Siren Synthesizer** (crafted using HTML5 Web Audio API oscillators without external mp3 dependencies).
  - Instant **GPS Geolocation Coordinates** detection with accuracy calculation.
  - **One-Click WhatsApp SOS Alert**: Sends distress message with exact Google Maps coordinates.
  - **One-Click SMS SOS Alert**: Pre-formatted emergency dispatch message.
- **Speed Dial Directory**:
  - `112` — National Emergency Response Support System (ERSS)
  - `1091` — Dedicated 24x7 Women Police Helpline
  - `181` — Domestic Violence & Abuse Prevention Helpline
  - `1098` — Childline (Girl child protection)
  - `1930` — Cyber Crime Helpline (Online harassment / financial fraud)
  - `108` — Emergency Ambulance Service
- **Essential Safety Apps**:
  - **Kavalan SOS** (Tamil Nadu Police - 3-second quick distress dispatch)
  - **112 India** (Ministry of Home Affairs ERSS)
  - **Himmat Plus** (Real-time transit tracker)
  - **Sakhi One Stop Centres (OSC)**

### 4. 🤖 Bharathi AI Chatbot & Voice Assistant
- **Voice Input (Speech Recognition)**: Users can click the microphone icon and speak in either **Tamil** or **English**.
- **Voice Output (Speech Synthesis)**: Speaks out responses with toggleable audio controls.
- **Knowledge Base**:
  - Detailed answers about Subramania Bharati's life, family, and exile in Pondicherry.
  - Explanations of poems on women's liberation.
  - Immediate advice on safety helplines, police procedures, and emergency actions.
  - Direct information on loans and higher education schemes.
  - Quick-prompt chips for instant one-tap queries.

### 5. 💼 Women Business Incubation & Career Portal
- **Business Proposal Application Form**:
  - Collects entrepreneur's details, sector (Handloom, AgriTech, AI & Tech, Handicrafts, EdTech, Healthcare, Retail), stage, and funding needed.
  - Automatically assesses the application, generates a unique ID (e.g., `TN-PENN-2026-XXXX`), and matches it to relevant government loan schemes.
- **Major Loan Schemes**:
  - **Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)**: Collateral-free loans (Shishu, Kishore, Tarun up to ₹10 Lakhs).
  - **Stand-Up India**: ₹10 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore for women greenfield enterprises.
  - **SHG Bank Linkage**: Revolving funds for women self-help groups.
- **Curated Jobs Portal**:
  - Filterable career listings for women across IT/Tech, Education, and Uniformed/Civil Services (TNUSRB Police Sub-Inspector, etc.).
  - Instant "Apply" modal with applicant data collection.

### 6. 🎓 Bharathi Kalvi Koodam (Learning Hub & Quiz)
- **Life Milestones Timeline**:
  - 1882: Child Prodigy in Ettayapuram
  - 1898: Varanasi/Kasi Awakening (Turban & Mustache)
  - 1906: Sister Nivedita Epiphany
  - 1908: Underground in Pondicherry
  - 1921+: The Eternal Legacy of Pudumai Penn
- **Interactive 5-Question Quiz**:
  - Real-time score counter and progress bar.
  - In-depth explanation of answers.
  - Digital **"Bharathi Pudumai Penn Scholar Badge"** upon completion.

### 7. 🎬 Media & Aesthetics
- **Video Documentary Tribute**: Embedded responsive documentary video of Bharathiyar's life and Penn Viduthalai compositions.
- **Fiery Embers Canvas**: Interactive background canvas generating rising sparks and embers symbolizing the torch of revolution.
- **Bharathiyar SVG Art Frame**: Handcrafted vector portrait of Mahakavi Bharathiyar with his iconic white turban, tilak, and fiery mustache.
- **Bilingual Toggle**: Seamless Tamil / English language switch across the entire website.

## 🏃 How to Run the Website

### Option 1: Direct Double-Click (Zero Setup)
Simply navigate to your Desktop folder:
`C:\Users\Syed\OneDrive\Desktop\Bharathiyar_Penn_Viduthalai`
and double-click **`index.html`** to open it in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Firefox.

### Option 2: Run via `start.bat` (Recommended for full Web Speech & HTTP features)
1. Double-click **`start.bat`**.
2. It will automatically boot Python's HTTP server at `http://localhost:8080` and open your default browser.
